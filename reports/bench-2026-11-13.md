## How to read this

- A result whose interval contains zero is not evidence either way.
- The four archetype opponents are generated by the same behaviour model the bot uses to predict opponents, so results against them overstate real-world strength. The independent rows are the meaningful ones.
- Nothing here measures play against humans or against other competition bots, and no live PokerNow hand is included.
