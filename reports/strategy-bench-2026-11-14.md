# Strategy benchmark: version 2 against the version 1 baseline

Generated by `sim/bot/strategy-bench.ts` on 2026-11-14, seed 2027, Node v24.20.0.
Version 1 is the strategy engine exactly as released in 0.5.1 (`engine: 'v1'`). Version 2 is the current engine.
400 Monte Carlo deals per postflop decision, 100bb stacks, blinds 10/20, cash mode, duplicate rotation. All figures are big blinds per 100 hands with a 95% confidence interval.
Seed 2027 was not used during development; pilot runs used other seeds.

## How to read this

- An interval that contains zero is not evidence either way.
- Every opponent here is a program. Nothing measures play against people or against other competition bots, and no solver reference is included.
- The simulated opponents do not punish the things version 2 was changed to avoid (for example, none of the v1-family opponents attacks a weak lead), so a simulation can understate or overstate the effect of those changes at a real table.
